BUNKER RUN

VR extraction shooter. Headset only.

Checking for a VR headset...

  • You always spawn in the bunker with only a bag. Red button = deploy into the city. Green beacons = extract.
  • Guns need a rack to chamber the first round: other-hand trigger on the bolt, pull it back, let it snap forward. Only bolt-action rifles need racking after every shot.
  • Grip the front of a gun with your other hand to aim two-handed and cut recoil. Push the mag into the magwell with your hand (it follows the gun's real insertion path and clicks in).
  • New: Steel hatches are hidden around every map - grab the yellow handle, lift the lid, and step into the hole to drop into an underground bunker. Find the 4-digit code on the west-room wall, point at the keypad (north wall of the first room) and pull the trigger to enter it and disarm the laser grid (lasers hurt and sound the alarm), grab the 2 glowing orange fuses from the tables in the side rooms and hold them to the fuse box next to the keypad to open the vault door, loot the vault, then stand on the green ring to climb out. Gunsmith bench: build guns part by part (incomplete guns can't fire). Palm a mag: click the left/right stick near a mag pouch. Keycards are very rare in crates.
  • You start with $250,000 and starter gear. Your left wrist watch shows health, raid time and loot value. Aim at any menu board from a distance and pull the trigger to click. Left Y button = settings. Three maps: City (Warden), Port (Admiral) and the close-quarters Factory (Foreman, AA-12 slugs). Find red, yellow and purple keycards for the vaults. Point your gun straight up to sprint (costs stamina, food and water - eat and drink by holding items to your mouth and pulling the trigger).
  • LMGs: grab the top cover with your free hand and lift it, drop the ammo belt into the feed tray from above, close the cover, then rack. Sights, suppressors, grips and lasers each change how the gun handles.
  • Mag loader in the bunker: ammo box on the left pad, magazine on the right pad, and it fills the mag.
  • The Warden (central compound) is huge and has guards circling him. He drops the M249, Tier 6 armor and a Tier 6 helmet.
  • Die or quit mid-raid and everything you carried is lost.
